# Installed DALY-Family BMS

Registry ID: `component-daly-bms-01`

Two incompatible identities exist: a 4S 12 V 30 A smart BMS and an active
balance model described as `R24TK1A-17S100A`. The retained DALY PDF is a family
manual, not proof of the installed current rating, cell-count range, connectors,
or communications options.

Photograph or transcribe the complete label and connectors before applying a
wiring diagram, accessory cable, application setting, or protocol. Do not
disconnect sampling leads just to identify the unit, and do not assume BMS
presence makes an excessive charger current safe.
