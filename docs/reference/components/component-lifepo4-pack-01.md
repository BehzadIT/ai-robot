# 4S2P LiFePO4 Battery Pack

Registry ID: `component-lifepo4-pack-01`

The pack is owner-reported as eight 18650 LiFePO4 cells in 4S2P, nominally
12.8 V and 4.4 Ah. Exact installed cells, construction revision, insulation,
interconnects, temperature sensing, fuse, and disconnect are unverified.

The retained Goldencell specification applies only if the installed cells match
its exact model. Do not unwrap or dismantle the pack solely to improve
documentation. Prefer construction/purchase records and safely visible labels.

Charging remains blocked until cell, BMS, charger, protection, selected mode,
and charge-current compatibility are established. The candidate cell document
and candidate 5 A charger create a material current-limit question; the BMS
must not be assumed to regulate charging current.
