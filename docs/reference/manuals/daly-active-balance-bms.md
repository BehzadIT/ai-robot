# DALY Smart Active Balance BMS Family Manual

Source: `source-daly-active-balance-manual`.

## Project-relevant facts

- The manual covers a family spanning multiple series counts and current
  ratings; it is not a single-model datasheet (page 1 and specification table).
- Sampling-wire installation starts at total negative and proceeds through
  successive cell positives; voltage order must be checked before inserting
  the BMS connector (section II.1).
- Accessory and sampling cables from different manufacturers are not declared
  interchangeable (section II.4).
- Battery type and capacity require configuration; family defaults do not
  prove the installed unit's settings (section II.3).
- The manual warns against direct thermal contact between the BMS heat surface
  and cells and notes that the enclosure may conduct (section II.4).

## Applicability limit

Do not use this family manual to select cell count, current rating, torque,
cable, connector, application setting, or communications protocol until
`Q-US002-001` identifies the installed model and matching accessories.
