# Panther iQLoad EVO Charger Manual

Source: `source-iqload-evo-manual`, 2021 English manual.

## Project-relevant facts

- The EVO 5 supports a selected LiFePO4 mode and lists up to 5 A at 12 V
  (technical data, page 9).
- The described LiFePO4 main phase reaches 14.6 V, followed by a limited-current
  saturation phase (charging-cycle description, pages 11-12).
- The manual describes rest/monitoring and a later limited repeat-charge or
  trickle phase when voltage falls (charging levels 7-9, page 12).
- The display and mode-selection instructions distinguish LiFePO4 from lead,
  AGM, winter, and motorcycle modes (display/mode section, pages 14-16).
- The manual requires reading battery instructions, ventilation, inspection,
  correct polarity, and rejection of damaged/frozen/non-rechargeable batteries
  (general safety and before-use sections).

## Applicability limit

The physical charger model and selected program remain unverified. The manual's
maintenance behavior conflicts with the project note saying trickle is
disabled. Do not charge until `Q-US002-007`, `Q-US002-014`, `Q-US002-017`, and
`Q-US002-018` are resolved sufficiently for a reviewed procedure.
