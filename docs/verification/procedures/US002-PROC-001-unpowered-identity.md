# US002-PROC-001: Unpowered Identity And Overview Capture

Version: 1

## Goal

Identify externally visible control and power hardware without energizing,
disconnecting sampling leads, opening the battery pack, or moving wiring.

## Why this is needed

Exact labels determine which manuals, ratings, connectors, and procedures may
apply. Visual similarity is insufficient.

## Tools and protective equipment

- Camera with adequate focus and lighting.
- Eye protection when working around the assembled chassis.
- Nonconductive pointer or labels; do not place metal tools across terminals.

## Safe setup

Robot off, charger disconnected from mains and pack, motor-power disconnect
open if its operation is already known, tracks stationary, and exposed power
terminals protected. Do not alter an unknown BMS sampling harness.

## Procedure

1. Capture a full robot overview and the power/control layout before touching
   anything.
2. Capture front, rear, connector, and label views of the BMS, charger, Pico,
   ESP32, Cytron driver, and both motors where safely visible.
3. Capture fuse, switch, disconnect, connectors, conductor markings, and cable
   routing without moving an unsafe or strained connection.
4. Transcribe only text that is legible in the retained photograph.
5. Hash originals, create annotated derivatives separately, and populate one
   evidence bundle.

## Expected result and tolerance

Each claimed identity is supported by legible markings or remains explicitly
unknown. No electrical tolerance applies.

## Failure indications

Unclear labels, inaccessible hardware, damaged insulation, swollen or hot
battery components, exposed conductors, or a need to dismantle the pack.

## Stop conditions

Stop immediately for heat, odor, swelling, damaged high-current wiring,
unexpected power indication, or any need to disturb an unidentified battery or
BMS connection.

## How the result changes the decision

Legible exact identity permits selecting candidate manuals and designing later
unpowered checks. It does not authorize powered work.

## Evidence to retain

Original and annotated photos, transcription, operator, time, safe-state
confirmation, affected component/question IDs, and file hashes.
