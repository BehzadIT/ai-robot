# Planning Checklist

Preserve ownership. Inspect docs, AGENTS, analogues, tests, and Git. Give every
task dependencies, repositories, subsystems, AC mapping, test mode, evidence,
doubles, and parallel safety. Reject cycles and uncovered criteria. Physical
tasks require approval, safe setup, stop conditions, expected result, and
append-only evidence. Ready means definition and plan have both been reviewed.

Distinguish research gaps, routine agent decisions, owner decisions, and
approval gates. Before asking, present evidence, consequences, recommendation,
uncertainty, and readiness effect; group only decisions sharing evidence and
consequences. Persist each answer and rationale. The handoff reports approach,
task graph, AC coverage, verification, gates, residual uncertainty, next action,
and whether any owner decision remains.
