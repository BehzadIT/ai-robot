# Refine Story

Use the [workflow](../../../docs/backlog/workflow.md), definition
[template](../../../docs/backlog/templates/story/README.md), and
[checklist](references/refinement-checklist.md). Inspect relevant context, play
back current and target behavior, and ask only contextual decision-changing
questions. Number outcomes `AC-NNN`; identify evidence, physical gates, learning,
and diagrams. Leave the story Draft and hand a complete definition to
`plan-story`, or explicitly remain Draft, split, Block, or Defer it.
