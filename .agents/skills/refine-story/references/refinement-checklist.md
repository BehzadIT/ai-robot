# Definition Refinement Checklist

Confirm user/value, present and target behavior, owner/boundaries, non-goals,
blocks/informs dependencies, risk, assumptions, owner questions, numbered
`AC-NNN` outcomes, evidence depth, approval/stop needs, learning, and diagrams.

Before asking, confirm the gap is not discoverable and is consequential under
the human-agent contract. Explain context, evidence, consequences,
recommendation, and readiness effect; batch only tightly related decisions and
persist each answer. At handoff, report coverage, boundary, decisions, evidence,
remaining uncertainty, next action, and whether any owner decision remains.
